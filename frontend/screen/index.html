          Setup

            Welcome

            Let's get J.A.R.V.I.S ready. It takes about five minutes.

            • Everything it uses is free. You'll make three free accounts and copy a key from each.
            • Keys are kept in the Windows Credential Manager, never in a file and never on this screen.
            • J.A.R.V.I.S never records you. Only what you say after "hey jarvis" is sent, to be turned into text.
            • Anything risky, like deleting a file, needs you to press a key first.

            You can skip any step and come back later: Settings, then "Open the setup guide".

            Keys

            For each service: press "Get a free key", sign in, create a key and copy it. Then press "Add key" and paste it into the small window that opens. It's shown as dots and saved straight into the Credential Manager.

            Gemini the AI that understands you
            Groq turns your speech into text
            Fish Audio J.A.R.V.I.S's voice

            Models

            Small files that let J.A.R.V.I.S hear "hey jarvis" and recognise your voice on this laptop (about 30 MB). Each one is checked against its fingerprint before it is saved.

            Microphone

            Pick the microphone J.A.R.V.I.S listens with, then press Test and say something. The test only measures how loud it is; nothing is recorded.

            Your voice

            Teach J.A.R.V.I.S your "hey jarvis" so only you can wake it. You say it 20 times, at your own pace. Each one becomes numbers straight away; no sound is kept.

            Start with Windows

            Start J.A.R.V.I.S by itself when you sign in. This adds one shortcut to your own Startup folder: no admin rights, and you can turn it off here or in Task Manager.
